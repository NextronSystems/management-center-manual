Scheduled Group Scan
====================

The Scheduled Group Scan section shows all scans that run repeatedly,
including their schedule. Group scans started by the scheduler appear at
the top of the Group Scan section when they start. Create new scheduled
tasks by clicking the ``Add Scheduled Group Scan`` button.
