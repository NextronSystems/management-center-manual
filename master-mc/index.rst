Master Management Center
========================

Master Management Center is a central management console for controlling multiple
Management Centers. It lets you manage controlled scans across your Management
Centers systems and provides one central point for Response Playbooks, Evidence
Collection, and IOC Management. A dedicated license is required.

.. note::
    Master Management Center is a separate system from your existing Management Center.
    This means a new server or VM and a dedicated license are required.
