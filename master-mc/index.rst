Master Management Center
========================

The Master Management Center is a single central management console that
can control all of your Management Centers. It is meant to centrally
manage controlled scans on all your Management Centers. The Master
Management Center also provides one central point of management for your
Response Playbooks, Evidence Collection and IOC Management. A special
license for this is needed.

.. note::
    Please note that the Master Management Center is a completely separate system from
    your existing Management Center. This means a new server/vm and a special
    license are required.
