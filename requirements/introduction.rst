Introduction
============

ASGARD Management Center is the central management platform for
`THOR <https://thor-manual.nextron-systems.com>`_ scans,
`Aurora <https://aurora-agent-manual.nextron-systems.com>`_
real-time endpoint protection, custom playbooks, and Thunderstorm.
It can manage distributed THOR scans on thousands of systems and
collect and forward scan results to
`ASGARD Analysis Cockpit <https://analysis-cockpit-manual.nextron-systems.com>`_
or your own SIEM.

ASGARD Management Center can control and execute complex response tasks
when needed, including opening live remote command prompts on endpoints
and running other incident response actions. It also provides an
easy-to-use interface for creating custom multi-step response playbooks,
which can execute commands on endpoints and collect the corresponding output.

The ASGARD Management Center comes bundled with Debian on a custom ISO file
and can be deployed in many different ways.

This document describes the functions and steps for setting up and operating
ASGARD Management Center. It also explains how to add systems for scanning
and run individual or group scans with separate parameters.
